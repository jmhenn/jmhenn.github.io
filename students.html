For students

Source material on the research topics of our group — a starting point for MSc and PhD students, and for anyone preparing to join us. For how to apply, see Join & collaborate.

Textbook

  • Scattering Amplitudes in Gauge Theories
    J. M. Henn, J. C. Plefka — Springer Lecture Notes in Physics. A pedagogical introduction to modern amplitude methods, with a dedicated website (errata, exercises, and supplementary material).
