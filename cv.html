Curriculum vitae

Theoretical physicist with an MPA in science management. Experience in international research cooperation and science policy. Download the short CV (PDF).

Professional experience

since 2018
Director, Max Planck Institute for Physics (MPP), Munich/Garching, and Honorary Professor, Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München.
2015–2018
Full Professor (W3) of theoretical physics, Johannes Gutenberg University Mainz.
2011–2015
Senior postdoctoral member, Institute for Advanced Study, Princeton, USA.
2008–2011
Postdoctoral associate, Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin.
2005–2008
PhD in theoretical physics, Université Lyon 1 (Claude Bernard), France — research carried out at the Laboratoire d'Annecy-le-Vieux de Physique Théorique (LAPTh).
